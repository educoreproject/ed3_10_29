For context

Of every 100 U.S. ninth-graders, roughly:
87 graduate high school on time
54 enroll in college the next fall
32 earn a college credential within six years
68 reach the workforce without one
At high-poverty high schools, about 25 of 100 graduates finish a degree within six years.
Themes are matched by keyword, so read them as a first pass rather than a coding of the answers. Context figures are rounded national approximations (NCES; National Student Clearinghouse). Form layout after C. J. Minard's 1869 flow map of Napoleon's Russian campaign.
